Company Work From Home Policy

Employees are allowed to work from home up to two days per week with manager approval.

Work from home is available for employees whose role can be performed remotely.

Employees must be available during core working hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Employees must attend scheduled meetings, respond to important messages, and complete assigned work on time.

Work from home is not automatically allowed for all roles. Some roles may require office presence depending on business needs, team requirements, or security reasons.

Employees should inform their manager in advance before working from home.

Long-term or full-time remote work requires approval from HR and the reporting manager.
